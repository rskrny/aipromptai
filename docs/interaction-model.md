# HelloTalk Interaction Model & Playbook

How to actually get more interactions on HelloTalk. This is the practical guide.

---

## The Engagement Funnel

```
Discovery/Search → View Profile → Send Message → Conversation → Language Exchange
       ↑                                              |
       |                                              v
  Moments ←── Corrections ←── See their post in feed
```

There are two paths: people find YOU, or you find THEM. Both matter.

---

## Part 1: Get People to Message You (Inbound)

### Profile Optimization (Do This First)

1. **Clear profile photo** — no sunglasses, no group pics, face visible
2. **Write a real introduction** — profiles with no info get skipped. Mention what you're interested in, why you're learning the language, what you can help with
3. **List specific hobbies and interests** — the algorithm uses these for matching, and people scan them before messaging
4. **Add a voice intro** — shows commitment, helps people hear your accent, stands out
5. **Set accurate CEFR level** — too high = intimidating partners, too low = mismatched conversations
6. **Keep "Show Online Status" ON** — people are more likely to message someone who's online right now
7. **Don't hide from search** — Me > Settings > Privacy > "Who Can Find Me" — make sure this isn't set to hide you

### Moments Strategy (Your Secret Weapon)

Moments are the highest-leverage thing you can do. Here's why:

- When you post in your target language, native speakers see it and can correct you
- Correcting your post = them interacting with you = often leads to a chat
- Moments ranked by engagement, so popular posts get seen by more people
- It's passive — you post once, and people come to you

**How to post effective Moments:**
1. **Always include an image** — image posts get dramatically more engagement
2. **Write in your target language** — this is what the community rewards
3. **Add "please correct me" tag** — explicitly invites corrections
4. **Add "please help me sound more natural"** — gets quality feedback
5. **Post something personal or interesting** — NOT "does anyone want to practice?" (everyone ignores these)
6. **Use Topic tags** — puts your post in specific feeds where interested people browse
7. **Post 2-3 times per day** — enough to stay visible without being spammy
8. **Post when your target language's speakers are awake** — timing matters

### Corrections Strategy (Build Reputation)

- **Correct other people's Moments** — this is the #1 way to start organic conversations
- Corrections earn you badges (like "Language Ambassador") displayed on your profile
- Badges signal you're a quality partner — serious users look for these
- Free accounts get 10 grammar corrections before hitting the PLUS paywall

### Live Rooms & Voicerooms

- Join rooms to practice speaking and get exposure to many users at once
- Hosting rooms = maximum visibility (your room appears in the Live section)
- Free: 90 minutes/day, VIP: unlimited
- If you host well, viewers send gifts (which convert to real money)

## Part 2: Start More Conversations (Outbound)

### First Messages That Actually Work

**Never send:**
- "Hi"
- "Hello, let's practice"
- "Do you want to be language partners?"

**Instead, send:**
- Reference something from their profile: "I saw you're into [hobby] — I am too! How do you say [something related] in [their language]?"
- Ask about their city/region: "I've always wanted to visit [their city]. What's your favorite thing about living there?"
- Comment on their Moment: "I loved your post about [topic]. I tried to write something similar..."

### Where to Find Good Partners

1. **Moments feed** — find people who are actively posting and engaged
2. **Discovery feed** — filter by language, use interest tags
3. **Live rooms** — chat with people after a room session
4. **Corrections** — correct someone's post, they thank you, conversation starts naturally

### Messaging Tips
- **Use voice messages** — shows effort, helps both of you practice pronunciation
- **Lead the conversation** since you started it — have questions and topics ready
- **Keep it balanced** — practice both languages, don't just use theirs
- **Be patient** — not everyone responds immediately, don't send follow-ups too quickly

## Part 3: Keep Conversations Going

- Ask open-ended questions about their culture, daily life, opinions
- Share photos of your city, food, experiences
- Offer to correct their messages (reciprocity)
- Set a regular practice schedule ("let's chat every Tuesday?")
- Use the voice/video call feature for deeper connection

## Quick Daily Routine for Maximum Interactions

1. **Morning**: Post a Moment with an image in your target language (add correction tags)
2. **Browse Moments**: Spend 10 min correcting other people's posts (aim for 5-10 corrections)
3. **Check Discovery**: Send 3-5 personalized first messages to interesting profiles
4. **Afternoon**: Post another Moment, reply to any comments/corrections on your morning post
5. **Evening**: Join a Live room or Voiceroom for 20-30 min
6. **Before bed**: Reply to all pending messages, post one more Moment if you have something to share
