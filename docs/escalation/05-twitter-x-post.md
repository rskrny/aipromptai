## Screenshots to attach

Pick 2 or 3 of these. Images make the post far more likely to spread and be taken seriously. Attach them to the single tweet, or to tweets 1 and 3 of the thread.

1. **The "system maintenance" screen** that was shown on the profile before April 11. If you took a screenshot of it while it was up, use that. It is the strongest single piece of evidence.
2. **The in-app "visibility boost" or "spotlight" purchase screen** as it currently appears for this account. Anything showing the app offering a paid boost product to the affected account.
3. **A Moments post** showing the low engagement pattern (around ten likes from followers, no stranger comments), ideally with the post date visible.
4. **The follower count** (~1,070) on the profile page, to establish that this is a long-standing real account and not a new complaint account.
5. **The profile page itself** after the April 11 unlock, to show the account is active and normal-looking.

Do not post the raw API error or internal error code on Twitter. Keep the technical specifics in the support email and the Apple report, where they help. On Twitter, plain language and screenshots do more work.
