# LinkedIn outreach to HelloTalk staff

## How to find the right people

Go to LinkedIn search and try each of these queries. Aim to message 2 or 3 people across different functions — one product, one trust and safety, one engineering or support lead. Do not mass-message everyone at once; space them out by a day or two.

Search terms that tend to surface the right profiles:

- `HelloTalk Product Manager`
- `HelloTalk Trust and Safety`
- `HelloTalk Community Operations`
- `HelloTalk User Experience`
- `HelloTalk Shenzhen` (the company is headquartered there; this catches people who list the city)
- `HelloTalk Engineering Manager`
- `Hello Talk` (with the space — some people write it that way)
- Company page: search "HelloTalk" as a company, then open "People" and filter by job function (Product, Operations, Engineering, Support)

Prioritize people whose profile lists a current HelloTalk role, ideally based in Shenzhen, Guangzhou, Hong Kong, or a US office. Senior individual contributors and mid-level managers tend to respond more often than executives or junior staff.
