# Apple "Report a Problem" submission

**URL:** https://reportaproblem.apple.com
**Category to choose:** "Request a refund" then "I didn't authorize this purchase" OR "Report a quality issue" — whichever path the form offers. If asked what the problem is, choose the closest match to "the app or feature did not work as advertised."

---

## Subject

Deceptive in-app purchases: HelloTalk sold visibility boosts to an account it had secretly removed from its discovery system

---

## Body (copy-paste)

I am reporting a pattern of deceptive in-app purchases and misrepresentation inside the HelloTalk app (developer: HelloTalk Learn Languages, available on the App Store).

I am a long-time user of HelloTalk with account ID 98755150 and approximately 1,070 followers. In early 2023, incoming messages to my account dropped from roughly 40 per day to between 0 and 2 per day, a drop of about 95 percent, with no warning, no policy violation notice, and no support communication.

On April 6, 2026, I was able to confirm the technical cause. HelloTalk's own search service returns an internal error (code 6000, "Get Search User Plan Failed") specifically for my account. In plain terms, my account has been silently excluded from the app's user discovery and search index entirely. It is not ranked low. It is not shown at all.

During the same period, and continuing today, the HelloTalk app has displayed and offered me paid "visibility boost" products inside the app. These products advertise that they will raise my profile's visibility in the discovery feed and search results. They cannot function as advertised for my account, because my account has been removed from the discovery and search system those products are supposed to target. HelloTalk continued to market and make these purchases available to me under that condition.

Separately, until April 11, 2026, my profile was locked behind a message framed as "system maintenance." That message was not system-wide. It targeted only my account. After I emailed HelloTalk support on April 6, 2026, the profile lock was quietly lifted on April 11, but the underlying search index exclusion has not been cleared.

I am asking Apple to review HelloTalk's in-app purchase practices under the App Store Review Guidelines covering accurate metadata, honest representations of functionality, and in-app purchases for services the developer knows the buyer cannot receive.

I am happy to provide the email correspondence with HelloTalk support, the technical error code, and timestamps on request.

Thank you for your time.
