# Trustpilot 1-star review

**URL:** https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hellotalk.com
**Rating:** 1 star

---

## Title

Silently removed from the app's discovery system for two years, told it was "system maintenance"

---

## Body (copy-paste)

I have been a HelloTalk user for years. My account (ID 98755150) has around 1,070 followers from the time when the app actually worked for me. I am an American learning Chinese and HelloTalk used to be the best tool I had.

In early 2023, my inbound messages dropped from about 40 per day to between zero and two per day. No warning, no violation, no explanation. I assumed it was an algorithm change and waited it out. It has now been roughly two years.

Earlier this month I finally got to the bottom of it. HelloTalk's own search service returns a specific internal error for my account that means my profile has been excluded from the app's user discovery index entirely. Not demoted, not shadow-limited, removed. On top of that, my profile had been locked behind a message claiming "system maintenance," but the maintenance message was not system-wide. It was targeted at my account only. Other users were unaffected.

After I emailed support, the profile lock was lifted. That part I appreciate. But the underlying exclusion from search and discovery was not cleared. My profile is editable again, and strangers still cannot find me. Zero discovery DMs. The only interactions I get on posts come from my existing followers.

Meanwhile, the app kept offering me paid visibility boost products the entire time, products that are supposed to raise my rank inside the very discovery system I had been secretly removed from.

If HelloTalk wants to penalize an account, it should say so and explain why. Hiding the penalty behind a fake maintenance screen for two years while continuing to sell visibility upgrades to the affected user is not acceptable. I am posting this review so other long-term users can recognize the same pattern if it has happened to them.
