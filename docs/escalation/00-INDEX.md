# Escalation package — read this first

This folder contains seven ready-to-use items designed to apply pressure from multiple directions at once so that HelloTalk has an incentive to restore your account to normal discovery status. Everything is written as a final product. You can copy and paste each item directly into the relevant site, app, or email client. No editing required except, in a couple of places, signing your name at the bottom.

## Suggested order of execution (fastest impact first)

The order below is designed to maximize the chance of a real response while minimizing risk. Start at the top and work down. You can pause at any point if HelloTalk resolves the issue.

| # | File | What it is | Where it goes | Estimated time |
|---|------|------------|---------------|----------------|
| 1 | `06-support-followup-email.md` | Firm but polite follow-up to HelloTalk support, referencing the April 6 email and the April 11 partial fix. | Email to support@hellotalk.com | 5 minutes |
| 2 | `01-apple-report-a-problem.md` | Formal complaint to Apple about deceptive in-app purchases being offered to an account excluded from the system the purchases target. | https://reportaproblem.apple.com | 10 minutes |
| 3 | `04-linkedin-dm-template.md` | Short, professional DM to 2 or 3 HelloTalk staff on LinkedIn, with search instructions for finding the right people. | LinkedIn | 20 to 30 minutes (mostly finding the right people) |
| 4 | `05-twitter-x-post.md` | One punchy tweet tagging @HelloTalkApp, plus a three-tweet thread version for more context, plus a list of suggested screenshots to attach. | X / Twitter | 10 minutes, plus time to gather screenshots |

Total active time, if you do everything: about 60 to 90 minutes, most of it spent finding LinkedIn contacts and gathering screenshots.

## How the pieces fit together

- Items 1 and 2 are the direct pressure moves. They go to the two parties that actually have the power to fix this: HelloTalk itself, and Apple, which controls HelloTalk's access to the App Store.
- Item 3 is the back-channel. Support queues often ignore normal users. A polite, specific LinkedIn message from someone who clearly knows the internal error code tends to get forwarded to the right person.
- Items 4, 5, and 6 are the public record. They matter less for immediate fix speed and more for creating a searchable trail. If HelloTalk stonewalls, these become the evidence base for any later journalist, regulator, or class complaint. They also create a small but real reputational cost to doing nothing.

## What to flag for me to confirm before sending

Before you send item 1 (Apple Report a Problem), confirm with me that you are filing from the US App Store. If you are in a different country, the form and the relevant consumer-protection framing may need a small adjustment.

Before you send items 4, 5, and 6 (LinkedIn, Twitter, Reddit), decide whether you want to use your real name or stay anonymous. The support email and the Apple report should use your real name in any case. The public items can be either.

## What not to do

- Do not post the raw error code 6000 or the "Get Search User Plan Failed" text on Twitter, Reddit, or Trustpilot. It belongs in the support email, the Apple report, and the LinkedIn DM, where technical specificity helps. In public posts, it looks like jargon and reduces credibility with non-technical readers.
- Do not accuse HelloTalk of intentional fraud in any of these messages. The facts speak for themselves. "The app continued to offer paid visibility boosts to an account that had been excluded from the visibility system" is stronger than any accusation you could make on top of it.
- Do not send everything in one day. Space the public posts out over 48 to 72 hours so HelloTalk sees a sustained signal rather than a single burst.

## If and when HelloTalk responds

If support replies with a real ticket number and an actual investigation, pause items 4, 5, and 6 and wait for the outcome. Keep item 2 (Apple) in play regardless, because the deceptive in-app purchase pattern is its own separate issue and worth documenting even if your personal account gets fixed.
