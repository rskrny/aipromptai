# HelloTalk Matching / Discovery Algorithm

## How Discovery Works

The discovery feed shows potential language partners. Here's what we know controls who shows up and in what order.

## Confirmed Factors

These are confirmed from official HelloTalk sources:

1. **Language pair** (required) — Must be complementary: you speak what they want to learn, and vice versa
2. **CEFR proficiency level** — HelloTalk's "Level Match" system pairs users at similar skill levels (self-reported)
3. **Location / region** — Nearby users can be prioritized (VIP-only filter: "nearby user search")
4. **Age** — Used as a filter in discovery
5. **Hobbies, interests, and goals** — Set during profile creation, used for matching
6. **Interest tags** — Specific tags you select affect who you're shown to
7. **VIP status** — VIP members get confirmed "extra profile exposure" and "optimized language partner matching"
8. **Privacy settings** — "Who Can Find Me" (Me > Settings > Privacy) can hide you from discovery for a week or month, or restrict to same-gender only

## What This Means for You

### To maximize who sees your profile:
- **Don't enable "Hide from Search"** — this removes you from discovery entirely
- **Keep "Show Online Status" on** — makes you appear active and approachable
- **Get VIP** — confirmed extra visibility in discovery results
- **Fill out all profile fields** — interests, hobbies, goals all feed into matching
- **Set accurate CEFR level** — if you set it too high or too low, you get mismatched partners

### What probably helps (not officially confirmed):
- Being recently active (online now or recently)
- Having a high response rate to messages
- Posting Moments regularly (signals engagement)
- Giving corrections (earns badges, signals quality partner)

## Moments Feed Algorithm (Confirmed)

This is separate from discovery but important for visibility:

- **Moments within a Topic are ranked by engagement (likes + comments), NOT by time posted**
- This means a post with lots of likes stays at the top of a Topic feed
- Early engagement snowballs — getting quick likes/comments pushes you higher
- Posts with images get significantly more engagement
